A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious private office in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake.

The office should feel powerful, controlled, and deliberately intimidating — a space designed to impress inspectors rather than to be lived in.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D perspective (no depth, no dramatic perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no cinematic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

large luxury writing desk with drawers

hidden safe built into the wall or behind a panel

tall bookcase with neatly arranged books

small cabinet under the desk

designer desk lamp

expensive executive chair

framed painting on the wall

display shelf with awards and trophies

abstract sculpture on a pedestal

The room should look suspiciously perfect, overly tidy, and carefully staged, as if prepared in advance for an official inspection.

Lighting is soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple, clean, and suitable for a 2D game background.
