A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious private collection room in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official.

The room is dedicated to rare collectibles and vintage luxury items, with a strong focus on a classic racing car from the 1930s.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie and sterile mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake. No realism, no photorealism.

The space should feel expensive, curated, and museum-like — more like a private exhibition than a functional room.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D side-view perspective

single flat back wall only (no visible side walls, no ceiling perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no cinematic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

vintage 1930s racing car (classic pre-war design, large wheels, exposed details, elegant silhouette)

glass display pedestal with rare collectible item

wall-mounted display shelves with luxury collectibles

framed historical racing posters or photographs

polished floor suitable for exhibition

The racing car should be the visual centerpiece, perfectly clean and staged, as if never driven.

The room should feel suspiciously perfect, curated for inspection rather than passion.

Lighting is very soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple, clean, and suitable for a 2D game background.
