A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious modern kitchen in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie and sterile mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake.

The kitchen should feel expensive, cold, and unused — more like a design showroom than a place where people actually cook.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D side-view perspective (no depth, no dramatic perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no cinematic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

upper kitchen cabinets (wall-mounted)

lower kitchen cabinets

large luxury refrigerator

built-in microwave

built-in oven

minimalist trash bin

clean kitchen table

sink with modern faucet

expensive coffee machine

The kitchen should look suspiciously perfect and overly clean, as if prepared in advance for an inspection and never actually used.

Lighting is soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no food, no dishes, no clutter.
No text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple, clean, and suitable for a 2D game background.
