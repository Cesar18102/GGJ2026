A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious private garage in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official, accessible via a private elevator.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie and sterile mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake. No realism.

The garage should feel expensive, pristine, and curated — more like a private car showroom than a functional garage.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D side-view perspective (no depth, no dramatic perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no cinematic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

luxury car (parked, perfectly clean)

visible car trunk area (closed, clearly readable as a separate zone)

glove compartment area inside the car

expensive motorcycle

wall-mounted cabinet for car accessories

The space should be immaculately clean, with polished floors and no tools, no oil stains, no signs of actual mechanical work.

Lighting is very soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no clutter, no text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple, clean, and suitable for a 2D game background.
