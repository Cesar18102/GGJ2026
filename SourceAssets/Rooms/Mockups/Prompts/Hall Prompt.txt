A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious entrance hall in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake.

The room should feel expensive, sterile, and overly clean — like a showroom rather than a lived-in home.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D perspective (no strong depth or extreme perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no dramatic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

luxury shoe cabinet with drawers

minimalist coat rack with a few expensive coats

small wall-mounted key drawer

tall designer vase

expensive rug on the floor

large frameless mirror on the wall

hat shelf

shoe shelf

decorative wall panel

The entrance hall should feel intentionally tidy and suspiciously perfect, as if prepared for an inspection.

Lighting is soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple and not textured heavily, suitable for a game background.
