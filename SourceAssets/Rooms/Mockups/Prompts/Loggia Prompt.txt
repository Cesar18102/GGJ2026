A 2D side-view interior scene of a luxurious enclosed balcony (loggia) in the home of a very wealthy corrupt official.

Style: dark stylized cartoon, hand-drawn look, thick outlines, flat colors, slightly eerie and sterile mood, similar to Don’t Starve Together and Rusty Lake. No realism.

The loggia should feel expensive, curated, and artificial — like a luxury relaxation space designed to look pleasant but rarely used.

Composition requirements:

flat 2D side-view perspective (no depth, no dramatic perspective)

clear separation between objects

no overlapping objects

each object visually distinct and easy to cut out as a separate sprite

no motion blur, no cinematic lighting, no particles

Objects in the room:

large decorative plant pots with indoor flowers

minimalist bar cabinet with expensive bottles

modern lounge chairs

The plants should look perfectly maintained and slightly artificial, as if cared for by staff rather than by the owner.

The space should feel calm but subtly unsettling, like a luxury hotel balcony prepared for inspection.

Lighting is very soft and even, neutral shadows only.

No people, no clutter, no personal items.
No text, no UI elements.

Background should be simple, clean, and suitable for a 2D game background.
